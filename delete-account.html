Delete your account and data

Cemaat Vakti · Elbiz Studio

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Cemaat Vakti and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

If you can’t open the app

From the email address on your account, write to elbizstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and sign-in data) and profile (name and icon)
  • Your prayer history, qada tracking, streak, badges and list of mosques
  • Your group memberships and notification settings

What may remain

Some older daily entries already shared in a group may remain in that group until the group is deleted. Email us and we will delete them within 30 days. We keep no backups.
