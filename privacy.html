3. Location

  • Your location is used and stored only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. It is not saved on our servers.
  • To fetch prayer times, only the number of the nearest district is sent, not your location.
  • When you use “Nearby mosques”, your approximate location at that moment is sent to the map services listed below so they can find mosques. They do not link it to you.
  • When you search for a mosque by name on the map, your search text is also sent to that map service.
  • Location permission is optional; you can pick your city from a list instead.

4. Who can see your data?

  • Your group friends: only members of the same group see your name, icon, your prayer status in that group, your streak, your badges (unless hidden), your reactions, and the khatm parts given to you in that group with the pages you marked.
  • Us: we never sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

5. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (accounts and database), with data stored on Google servers in Europe.
  • Expo (notification delivery and app updates): your push token, the notification text and, to refresh the home-screen widget, the group's summary for that day (members' names, icons and prayer marks) are delivered through this service, only to members of that group.
  • Google Cloud Functions (Europe, Belgium): our own server. It sends group notifications and for this reads the names, icons, that day’s prayer marks and push tokens of that group’s members. It also fetches the Diyanet prayer times.
  • Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet) Awqat Salah service (official prayer times): the app sends the district number to our own server on Google Cloud (Europe, Belgium); our server passes only the district number to Diyanet and stores the times for everyone. Your name, email or location are not sent to Diyanet.
  • ezanvakti.emushaf.net (fallback, prayer times based on the Diyanet calendar), used if our server is unreachable; only a district number is sent.
  • OpenStreetMap-based services (map tiles and mosque search: OpenStreetMap, Photon/komoot, Overpass servers) and unpkg for the map library.
  • If you open a Quran page, the Quran portal of Türkiye’s Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet) (kuran.diyanet.gov.tr) opens in your browser. Only the page number is sent there; your read marks stay with us. Diyanet’s own privacy terms apply there.
  • If you open directions to a mosque, Google Maps opens and Google’s own privacy terms apply.

International transfers: these providers’ servers are outside Türkiye (EU and USA). The transfer is based on the explicit consent you give when you start using the App.

The App currently contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools. If ads are added, this policy will be updated and any required permissions will be requested.

6. Legal basis

  • Account and group features: performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c).
  • Prayer records and international transfers: explicit consent (KVKK art. 6 and 9).

7. Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, profile, personal prayer history and group memberships are deleted immediately. Some older daily entries already shared in a group may remain in that group until the group is deleted; email us and we will delete them within 30 days.

How to delete your account: Account deletion

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, use it with your parent’s or guardian’s knowledge and permission.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules ensure people can change only their own data and see only groups they belong to. Notifications are sent only from our server; the event is verified on the server and the text is built from the real records. If you see an inappropriate name, icon or notification you can report and block the person in the app; reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Report records are deleted after review.

10. Your rights

You can ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, learn who it was shared with, object, and claim compensation. Write to elbizstudio@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Board.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will tell you in the App about important changes. The current version is always on this page.
