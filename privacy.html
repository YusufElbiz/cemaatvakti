3. Location

  • Your location is used and stored only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. It is not saved on our servers. The name of your place is also found from the list of provinces and districts on your phone; your location is not sent to Apple or Google for this.
  • The app does not check your location in the background. Your location only changes when you tap “Find my location” or pick a city.
  • To fetch prayer times, only the number of the nearest district is sent, not your location.
  • When you use “Nearby mosques”, your phone does not connect to map services directly: the search goes through our own server on Google Cloud (Europe, Belgium). Your location is rounded to about 1 km on your phone, and only this rounded location reaches the map services. Your IP address, name, account and exact location are not sent; what is sent cannot be linked to you.
  • When you search for a mosque or district by name, your search text is also passed on only through our server, without your identity. Map tiles also come from our server, and the map library is built into the app.
  • Location permission is optional; you can pick your city from a list instead.

4. Who can see your data?

  • Your group friends: only members of the same group see your name, icon, your prayer status in that group, your streak, your badges (unless hidden), your reactions, and the khatm parts given to you in that group with the pages you marked.
  • Us: we never sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

5. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (accounts and database): your prayer, fasting, group and profile data are stored on Google servers in Europe. Your email address and sign-in data (Firebase Authentication) are processed on Google servers in the United States.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service (iPhone): used to deliver notifications to your phone; these are each phone's own notification services, with no other company in between. Your push token, the notification text and, to refresh the home-screen widget, the group's summary for that day (members' names, icons and prayer marks) are sent from our server directly through these services, only to members of that group. Apple's own privacy terms also apply.
  • Google Cloud Functions (Europe, Belgium): our own server. It sends group notifications and for this reads the names, icons, that day’s prayer marks and push tokens of that group’s members. It also fetches the Diyanet prayer times.
  • Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet) Awqat Salah service (official prayer times): the app sends the district number to our own server on Google Cloud (Europe, Belgium); our server passes only the district number to Diyanet and stores the times for everyone. Your name, email or location are not sent to Diyanet.
  • ezanvakti.emushaf.net (fallback, prayer times based on the Diyanet calendar), used if our server is unreachable; only a district number is sent.
  • OpenStreetMap-based services (map tiles and mosque search: OpenStreetMap, Photon/komoot, Overpass servers, Europe): only our own server connects to them; your phone does not. The information sent cannot be linked to a person (rounded location, search text, map tile).
  • If you open a Quran page, the Quran portal of Türkiye’s Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet) (kuran.diyanet.gov.tr) opens in your browser. Only the page number is sent there; your read marks stay with us. Diyanet’s own privacy terms apply there.
  • If you open directions to a mosque, your phone’s map app opens (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android); only the mosque’s name and location are passed to it, and that company’s own privacy terms apply there.

International transfers: these providers’ servers are outside Türkiye (EU and USA). The transfer is based on the explicit consent you give when you start using the App.

The App currently contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools. If ads are added, this policy will be updated and any required permissions will be requested.

6. Legal basis

  • Account and group features: performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c).
  • Prayer and fasting records, menstruation/postnatal details, Hajj/Umrah statement and international transfers: explicit consent (KVKK art. 6 and 9).

7. Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your account, profile, prayer and fasting history, menstruation/postnatal details, badges, list of mosques, group memberships, your daily prayer entries in the groups you are a member of and the parts you took in other people’s khatms are deleted immediately. Older daily entries in groups you had already left, and the reactions you sent in groups (only your user ID and the reaction type, not your name), may remain until the group is deleted; email us and we will delete them within 30 days. Report records are deleted once the review is finished. We keep no backups.

How to delete your account: Account deletion

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, use it with your parent’s or guardian’s knowledge and permission.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules ensure people can change only their own data and see only groups they belong to. Notifications are sent only from our server; the event is verified on the server and the text is built from the real records. If you see an inappropriate name, icon or notification you can report and block the person in the app; reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Report records are deleted after review.

10. Your rights

You can ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, learn who it was shared with, object, and claim compensation. Write to elbizstudio@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Board.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will tell you in the App about important changes. The current version is always on this page.
